Training priorities

What matters most

1. Comfortable breathing. Practise steady exhalation underwater and relaxed inhalation. If congestion or a feeling of not getting enough air persists, seek medical advice before increasing distance.

2. Aerobic endurance. Keep most swimming at a controlled effort. Avoid turning every session into a time trial.

3. Open-water skills. Practise sighting and swimming straight in suitable conditions with appropriate supervision.

4. Safety and recovery. Do not swim alone in open water. Stop if you become unusually breathless, dizzy or develop chest pain.
