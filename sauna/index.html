How it works

An estimate based on your session.

The sauna estimate rises with each 10-minute round. Height and weight adjust it using an estimated body-surface-area ratio. Cold time adds a resting baseline only.

See the model assumptions
  • At 90°C, the model uses reported monitor estimates of 73.04, 93.82, 114.91 and 131.40 kcal for successive 10-minute sauna rounds.
  • At 80°C, the round estimates are scaled to a separate study’s 20-minute monitor estimate. Temperatures between 80°C and 90°C are interpolated.
  • Body size is scaled against the 90°C study’s average height and weight using a body-surface-area formula. The study found associations with weight and body surface area, not height alone.
  • Each sauna round includes a five-minute recovery estimate. Cold-bath time adds a resting baseline; no additional cold-response calories are assumed.
  • Age is collected but does not change this estimate. The study data don’t support an age adjustment.

These are heart-rate-monitor estimates from specific study groups, not direct calorimeter measurements. Individual results can differ.

Sources: 90°C repeated-round study, 80°C study, calorie units.
