A NOTE ON THE NUMBERS

A simple starting model.

These are invented prototype assumptions, not measured results or a validated calorie model. The table estimates extra kcal per 10 minutes at a reference height of 170 cm. Each 10 cm above or below 170 cm adds or subtracts 0.5 kcal. Session time scales linearly. Age is collected but is not used in this first table.
