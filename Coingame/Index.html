The Kelly benchmark

For a 60% chance to win even-money bets, the full Kelly fraction is:

f* = (bp − q) / b = (1 × 0.60 − 0.40) / 1 = 20%

Kelly maximises long-run expected logarithmic wealth under its assumptions. It does not maximise the chance of winning every session, and it can produce substantial drawdowns.

What the odds imply

For every $1 staked on Heads, expected net profit is:

0.60 × $1 − 0.40 × $1 = +$0.20

That is a 20% expected return on the amount staked per bet, before considering volatility.

Over 20 independent flips, the expected number of Heads is:

20 × 0.60 = 12 Heads

This is an average across many simulated games. Any single run can vary considerably.

This is an educational probability simulation, not a prediction about financial markets or a guarantee of profit. The 60/40 bias is assumed to be real and stable.
