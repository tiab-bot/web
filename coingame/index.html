TAKE RISK?

In investing, you face two broad choices: aim to get the outcome right more often by risking a smaller amount each time, or take larger positions that can produce bigger gains and losses.

Style A · Smaller stakes

Win more often.

Choose the outcome you think is more likely and put a smaller share of your portfolio at risk. This limits the impact of each mistake.

Style B · Larger stakes

Make each decision count.

Commit a larger share of your portfolio to each view. Correct calls have more impact, but incorrect calls cost more.

The maths behind the reference

The Kelly criterion.

The reference strategy bets 20% of its current portfolio on Heads every round. With a 60% chance of winning and an even-money payout, the full Kelly fraction is:

60% − 40% = 20%

Kelly is designed to maximise expected long-run logarithmic growth when the probabilities and payouts are known. It does not guarantee the highest balance over just 20 rounds. A short run can favour a different strategy.

This exercise is educational. It does not establish a clinical or scientifically validated personality type, and the simulated coin probabilities are known in advance.
